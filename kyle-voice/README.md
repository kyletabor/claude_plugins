# kyle-voice

My own writing rules for anything that goes out under my name or to my clients: posts, emails, intros, site pages, bios, proposals, client decks and docs.

Every rule comes from a correction I gave Claude on a real draft. It replaces the general-purpose writing and slide skills I was testing, which polished generic copy, oversold, and built decks for screen-shared talks instead of a PDF a client reads and forwards.

## What it does

- Starts from my own words and the reader's words, not a blank page.
- Keeps copy plain, honest and unsalesy: underpromise, no hype, no accusations, "AI" only as a tool.
- Cuts the patterns that read as AI (binary contrasts, dramatic reveals, cute labels, same-shaped titles).
- Sets the house style for client decks and docs: one theme, no animations, PDF-first, a © mark on every page, and an arc where each slide sets up the next.

Invoke: `/kyle-voice`, or ask for a draft, a deck or a proposal.

## Personal

This is tuned to one person. Fork it and swap in your own corrections; the structure (rules from real feedback, a pattern list, a house style) is the useful part.
